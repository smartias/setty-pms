How should 2c deliver the package?
2c. Package & Draft Email is the full client-send flow; pick the delivery method above first. Via Client Portal publishes the checked files + cover to a dated folder under Setty Deliverables and links there (no expiry — clients use their portal access). Email as attachment attaches the files directly to the draft (no portal needed — use this if the portal isn't set up or isn't cooperating; best for smaller sets). Zip + 30-day link bundles everything and shares a 30-day anonymous link (good for large sets with no portal). Either way an Outlook draft opens for review — nothing is sent automatically. 2a and 2b are partial alternatives if you only need the cover saved or a local zip.
