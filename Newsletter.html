3 · Create the draft

This creates draft(s) in Outlook with everyone on BCC (recipients can't see each other). Large lists are split into multiple drafts. Nothing sends automatically — you open each draft, review it, and click Send.
Smaller drafts, sent a few at a time over a day or two, are less likely to be treated as spam.
